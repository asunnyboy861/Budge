## StoreKit 2 Implementation Plan

### PurchaseManager.swift
- Product fetching: `Product.products(for:)` with product IDs
- Purchase flow: `product.purchase()` with `.subscribe` type
- Transaction listening: `Transaction.updates` listener
- Status tracking: `Product.SubscriptionInfo.Status` check
- Restore: `AppStore.sync()` call
- Entitlement check: `Transaction.currentEntitlements` verification

### Paywall UI
- Display monthly and yearly options side by side
- Highlight yearly savings (58%)
- Show free trial badge on monthly option
- Include "Restore Purchases" link
- No manipulative urgency or countdown timers
