# App Store Metadata

## Promotional Text
Track spending in seconds and stay on budget effortlessly. Smart trends, positive encouragement, and zero stress.

## Description
Budge is the simplest way to track your spending and stay on budget. No complicated setup, no financial jargon, no guilt trips. Just quick logging and clear insights that help you spend smarter.

WHY BUDGE?

Most budget apps overwhelm you with charts, accounts, and settings. Budge does one thing really well: helping you log expenses fast and see where you stand. Open the app, tap a category, enter an amount. Done in under 3 seconds.

KEY FEATURES

One-Tap Expense Logging
Record any expense in seconds with smart categories. Food, coffee, transport, shopping - just tap and go. No forms to fill, no accounts to link.

Budget Progress at a Glance
See exactly how much you have left this month. A clear progress bar and encouraging feedback keep you motivated without making you feel bad about spending.

90-Day Spending Trends
Visualize your spending patterns over time. Spot trends, see which categories are growing, and get personalized insights to improve your habits.

Smart Insights
Budge analyzes your spending patterns and gives you practical tips. Notice a category trending up? Budge will let you know with actionable suggestions.

Positive Encouragement
Unlike other apps that make budgeting feel like a punishment, Budge celebrates your wins. Staying under budget feels great, and Budge reminds you of that.

Custom Categories and Limits (Pro)
Create your own spending categories and set per-category budget limits for more granular control over your finances.

AI Deep Insights (Pro)
Get AI-powered analysis of your spending habits with personalized recommendations to save more.

Siri Shortcuts (Pro)
Log expenses hands-free with Siri. Just say "Hey Siri, log lunch" and it's done.

Advanced Widgets (Pro)
See your budget status right on your home screen and lock screen without opening the app.

Biometric Lock (Pro)
Keep your financial data private with Face ID or Touch ID protection.

iCloud Sync
Your data syncs seamlessly across all your iPhone and iPad devices via iCloud.

CSV Export
Export your transaction history as a CSV file for your records or to use in other tools.

Daily Reminders
Set a daily reminder to log your expenses so you never forget.

Budge is perfect for anyone who wants a simple, stress-free way to stay on top of their spending. Whether you are saving for a goal, paying off debt, or just want to be more mindful about where your money goes, Budge makes it easy.

Download Budge today and start your journey to smarter spending!
